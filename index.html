What exactly is an AI agent?

It is software that can read, decide and act on a task the way a person would. Unlike a simple chatbot, an agent actually does the work: it sends the email, updates the spreadsheet, books the meeting.

Do we need technical people on our side?

No. We handle the building, setup and maintenance. You tell us how the task works, we do the rest.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

We test every agent on your real work before launch, and anything important needs your approval first. Every action is logged, so issues are easy to spot and fix.

Will it replace my staff?

Agents take over the repetitive parts of a job so your people can focus on customers, decisions and growth.

Which tools can the agents work with?

Most common business tools, including Gmail, Outlook, Google Sheets, Excel, Slack, HubSpot, WhatsApp and Xero. If you use something else, ask us.

How long does it take to get an agent?

Most agents are live within a few weeks of our first conversation.

Do you build apps as well as agents?

Yes. We build custom internal apps such as client portals, CRMs, dashboards, inventory systems and intranets. Apps and agents work together on the same data, so an agent can update a portal while your team works in it.

Can our clients or suppliers log in?

Yes. Portals have secure logins and permissions, so each client, supplier or staff member only sees what they should.

Who owns the app and the data?

You do. We can set everything up under your own accounts, so your data and your app stay yours.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need built. Tell us about it and we will send you a clear quote, with no obligation.

What should we build for you?

Tell us a little about your business and what you need. We will come back within one business day to book your free discovery call.
