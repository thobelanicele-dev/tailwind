FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is an AI agent?

Software that reads, decides and acts on a task the way a person would. Unlike a chatbot, it does the work: it sends the email, updates the spreadsheet, books the meeting.

Do we need technical people on our side?

No. We handle the building, setup and maintenance. You tell us how the work is done, we do the rest.

What if an agent makes a mistake?

We test everything on your real work before launch, anything important waits for your approval, and every action is logged.

Who owns the app and the data?

You do. We can set everything up under your own accounts.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Tell us about it and we will send a clear, fixed quote, with no obligation.
